Every Cake is Fresh, Homemade, and Eggless

Operating from our home kitchen in Kokapet, we bake everything from scratch for your order. We specialize in detailed, personalized cakes that are completely eggless, without compromising on taste or texture. It's our passion to create something unique and delicious for your event.

Let's Bake Your Idea Into a Cake

Ordering your custom cake is simple. Just follow these steps to get started.

  1. Get in Touch

    Send a direct message (DM) to our Instagram or call us with your cake idea, theme, and the date of your celebration.

  2. Design & Details

    We'll chat about the design, flavours, and size to make sure we create the perfect cake for you.

  3. Confirm Your Order

    Once you're happy with the plan, we'll confirm your order and schedule it for baking.

  4. Collection

    Your freshly baked, custom cake will be ready for you to pick up in Kokapet, Hyderabad.
