Our Bakes

Sourdough Bread

Our naturally leavened sourdough is made with a 36-hour fermentation process for a deeper flavour and beautiful texture. It’s all about patience and simple, quality ingredients.

Classic Country Loaf

Our signature loaf with a crisp, beautifully caramelized crust and a soft, open crumb. Perfect for toast, sandwiches, or simply enjoyed on its own with a touch of butter.

Eggless Celebration Cakes

Baked to order for your special occasions. We believe every celebration deserves a unique cake, made with love and without eggs. We love working with you to create the perfect centerpiece.

Custom Birthday Cakes

From Our Kitchen to Yours

Mitti Bake House is a small home bakery born from a simple love for traditional baking. "Mitti" means earth or soil, reflecting our commitment to natural processes and wholesome ingredients. Every loaf and cake is crafted by hand in small batches. We believe in patience, letting our sourdough develop its full character over 36 hours, and creating cakes that are as honest as they are beautiful.
