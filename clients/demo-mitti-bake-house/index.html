Thirty-six hours from flour to loaf

We don't rush things here. Every sourdough loaf is naturally leavened and undergoes a 36-hour cold ferment. This slow process develops a deep, complex flavour and a digestible crumb, something you can't get from a factory. It's just simple ingredients, time, and care, from our home to yours.

Our Weekend Sourdough and Celebration Cakes

Naturally Leavened Sourdough

Our signature loaf, perfect for your weekend table. Made with a blend of whole wheat and refined flours for a soft crumb and a crisp, crackling crust.

Custom Eggless Cakes

Made to order for your birthdays, anniversaries, and special moments. Tell us your favourite flavours and we will create a beautiful, delicious, and entirely eggless cake for you.

Order Two Days Ahead for Weekend Collection

All our bakes are made fresh to order. To ensure we can bake for you, please get in touch at least two days before you'd like to collect. All orders are collected from our home in Kondapur, Hyderabad on weekends.
