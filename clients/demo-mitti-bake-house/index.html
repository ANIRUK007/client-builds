Our Weekend Bakes: Sourdough & Celebration Cakes

Naturally Leavened Sourdough

Made with a 36-hour slow ferment for a rich flavour and a healthy, open crumb. Perfect for toast, sandwiches, or just with a knob of butter.

Eggless Celebration Cakes

Custom cakes for your special occasions. I work with you to create a beautiful and delicious centrepiece for your celebration, always baked without eggs.
