From Our Home to Yours

We are a home bakery in Kondapur, not a factory. Every loaf and cake is mixed, shaped, and baked in our small-batch kitchen with care.

We believe in the slow way. Our sourdough develops its flavour over 36 hours, and every cake is baked to order just for you. This means we need at least two days' notice for any order.
